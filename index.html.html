About

Anna Levitcaia · Founder, Gorg Wear · Dubai, UAE
Most fashion brands are built when conditions are easy. Gorg Wear was built when everything stopped. I founded Gorg Wear in Dubai in 2019—right before global supply chains fractured and retail faced unprecedented disruption. Instead of waiting for ideal conditions, we leaned into resilience, operational grit, and a simple conviction: clothing should help people stay true to themselves while navigating real-world obstacles. Today, Gorg Wear is a globally recognized apparel line proudly made in the UAE, delivering high-quality, authenticity-driven pieces to customers worldwide. WHAT WE STAND FOR AT GORG WEAR: → Authenticity Over Hype: Designing meaningful garments that empower individuals to own their story and pursue their goals. → Proudly Made in the UAE: Upholding strict local craftsmanship and quality standards from Dubai to doorsteps across the globe. → Built Through Resilience: Turning supply chain constraints and market shifts into disciplined, customer-first operations. Over the past 7+ years as Founder, my focus has been bridging creative brand identity with end-to-end business ownership—from product development and resilient sourcing to international fulfillment. Whether you're a retail partner, creative collaborator, fellow founder in the UAE, or someone who values purpose-built fashion, I'd love to connect. Reach out here on LinkedIn or email me directly at anna@g-f.ae.
